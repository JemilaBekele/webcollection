Your first visit

What happens when you come in

A new-patient visit takes about 60 minutes. Nothing is done without your say-so.

Step 1 · 10 min

Talk it through

Tell us what's bothering you, your history, and anything that makes you nervous.

Step 2 · 15 min

Scan & X-ray

Low-dose digital X-rays and a 3D intraoral scan. You see them on screen with us.

Check & clean

Full exam, gum measurements, scale and polish by our hygienist.

A written treatment plan with prices and options. Take it home and think it over.

Questions

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans and file the claim for you. Send us your card before your visit and we'll confirm your cover.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book. We offer longer appointments, a stop signal you control, noise-cancelling headphones, and sedation when needed.

What counts as an emergency?

Severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or bleeding that won't stop. Call us and we'll see you the same day.

How often should children come in?

Every six months from their first tooth or first birthday, whichever comes first.
