What we do

Two directions, one supply chain

We export Ethiopian specialty and commercial grades, and we handle the import side for buyers who want one partner from farm gate to their warehouse door.

Green coffee export

Specialty Grade 1–2 and commercial Grade 3–5, washed and natural, traceable to the washing station.

Farm to port

How a lot reaches you

Every bag carries a lot number you can trace back to the washing station and the day it was processed.

Step 1 · Oct – Jan

Hand picking

Only red, ripe cherries. Pickers return to each tree three or four times a season.

Step 2 · 2 – 6 weeks

Washing & drying

Washed lots ferment 36–48 hours. Naturals dry whole on raised African beds to 11% moisture.

Step 3 · Addis Ababa

Grade, cup & ship

Hulled, screen-sorted to 14+, defect-counted and cupped by our Q graders before bagging.
